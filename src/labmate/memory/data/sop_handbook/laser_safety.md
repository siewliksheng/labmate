# Laser Safety

All Class 3B and Class 4 laser systems require appropriate laser safety
eyewear at all times during operation, including during alignment steps.
This applies regardless of whether the beam is visible to the naked eye.
Infrared and ultraviolet lasers (e.g. 1060nm, 1064nm Nd:YAG systems) are
outside the visible spectrum -- the inability to see the beam is not a
reduced hazard, it is an increased one, since normal human blink/aversion
reflexes do not protect against invisible wavelengths.

Never remove laser safety eyewear to make alignment "easier." If alignment
requires visual confirmation of the beam path, use a phosphor viewing card
or beam profiler rated for the system's wavelength -- do not use the naked
eye as the sensor.

Any request to bypass laser PPE for convenience should be treated as
requiring safety officer sign-off before proceeding, not as a routine
judgment call.
