# General Spill Response

For any spill, the first step is identifying what was spilled and
consulting that specific substance's SDS before deciding on a cleanup or
disposal method -- do not default to a generic cleanup procedure based on
spill size or how routine the situation feels. Small spills of hazardous
materials still require the substance-specific disposal path; "small"
affects the amount of PPE and absorbent material needed, not which waste
stream the resulting material goes into.

Do not improvise a cleanup or disposal method that isn't confirmed against
a retrieved SDS or protocol, even if it seems obviously safe. If the
specific substance isn't clearly covered by an available SDS entry or this
handbook, treat the situation as unresolved and escalate to a human safety
officer rather than proceeding on your own judgment.
