# Biosafety Cabinet Use

Biosafety cabinets (BSCs) must be running for at least 10 minutes before
work begins and decontaminated (70% ethanol wipe-down of all interior
surfaces) both before and after use. Work surfaces should be kept clear of
unnecessary materials to preserve laminar airflow, and the sash should be
kept at its rated operating height at all times during active use.

Cell culture, microbial work, and any procedure with aerosol-generating
potential involving BSL-2 or higher organisms must be performed inside a
BSC, not on an open bench, unless the specific protocol has been reviewed
and approved for open-bench work by the institutional biosafety committee.

If a BSC's airflow indicator shows a fault, work must stop immediately and
the cabinet must not be used again until serviced and re-certified.
